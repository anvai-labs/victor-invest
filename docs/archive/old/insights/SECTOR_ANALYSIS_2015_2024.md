# Sector Analysis (2015-2024)

**Data Source:** SEC Company Facts + market data
**Methodology:** Median with 5th-95th percentile filtering
**Analysis Date:** February 2025

---

## Executive Summary

The past decade spans three distinct market regimes:

| Regime | Period | Characteristics | Fed Funds |
|--------|--------|----------------|-----------|
| **Pre-COVID** | 2015-2019 | Low volatility, steady growth | 0.25% → 2.50% |
| **COVID Bubble** | 2020-2021 | Extreme volatility, policy-driven | 0% → 0.25% |
| **Inflation & Rates** | 2022-2024 | Multiple compression, value rotation | 0% → 5.25% |

**Key Finding:** Technology P/E compressed 59% (116x → 48x), Energy compressed 67% (42x → 14x). Most sectors now trade at or below 10-year averages.

## Sector-by-Sector Analysis

### Technology: Bubble & Correction
```
P/E: 116x → 37x → 48x (2016 → 2020 → 2024)
- 2016-2019: 108-133x (elevated pre-COVID)
- 2020: 37x (COVID crash)
- 2021: 57x (recovery peak)
- 2022-2024: 56-48x (Fed hikes, compression)
```
**Assessment:** 48x is reasonable for quality mega-caps (AAPL, MSFT, GOOGL, NVDA).

### Healthcare: Steady Compression
```
P/E: 72x → 31x (2016 → 2024)
- Consistent decline over decade
- Current: 31x (attractive vs history)
```

### Energy: Extreme Cyclicality
```
P/E: 42x → 7x → 14x (2016 → 2022 → 2024)
- 2022: 7x (Ukraine war + rate hikes bottom)
- 2024: 14x (recovery from lows)
```

### Financials: Value Play
```
P/E: 18x → 14x (-22%, 2016-2024)
- Post-GFC recovery
- 2023 banking stress
- Quality vs weak banks diverging
```

### Consumer Discretionary
```
P/E: 41x → 29x (-29%)
- Amazon/Tesla weight on index
- P/S: 4.2x → 1.8x (-62%)
```

## Key Frameworks

### Balloon Effect
- **Inflating:** Earnings +100% → Price +50% → Multiple EXPANDS → Price +200% (NVDA 2023-24)
- **Deflating:** Earnings +50% → Price -20% → Multiple COMPRESSES → Price -40% (META 2021-23)
- **Stubborn:** Earnings +150% → Price +150% → Multiple FLAT (AAPL 2019-24)

### Investment Implications

**For Value Investors:**
- Technology: 48x P/E reasonable for quality
- Healthcare: 31x attractive vs history
- Energy: 14x at cycle bottom (high risk/reward)
- Financials: 14x with improving fundamentals

**For Growth Investors:**
- Tech multiple compression mostly complete (73x → 48x)
- AI beneficiaries showing expansion (NVDA, AMD)
- Biotech recovery possible from Healthcare lows

**Macro Outlook:**
- Rate cuts in 2025 = growth multiple relief
- Quality mega-caps maintain premium
- Small caps poised for rebound if rates decline
