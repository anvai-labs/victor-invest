# Sector Multiples Timeline Analysis (2016-2025)

## Executive Summary

This analysis tracks valuation multiples (P/E, P/S, P/B) across Technology sector and related industries from fiscal year 2016 to 2025 - a **10-year period** capturing the full market cycle including:
- Pre-pandemic peaks (2016-2017)
- Pandemic boom (2020)
- Growth stock bubble (2020-2021)
- Inflation/rate hike cycle (2022-2023)
- AI resurgence and recovery (2024-2025)

## Key Findings

### Technology Sector (10-Year Overview)

| Metric | 2016 | 2017 | 2019 | 2020 | 2022 | 2024 | 2025 | 2016→2025 Change | Trend |
|--------|------|------|------|------|------|------|------|------------------|-------|
| **P/E** | 116.4x | 132.5x (peak) | 94.8x | 73.5x | 55.7x | 53.4x | 60.9x | **-47.7%** | SHRINKING |
| **P/S** | 17.2x | 14.4x | 14.1x | 11.3x | 9.8x | 8.7x | 8.4x | **-50.8%** | SHRINKING |
| **P/B** | 16.0x | 14.3x | 11.5x | 11.2x | 10.2x | 9.2x | 8.9x | **-44.5%** | SHRINKING |

**Interpretation**: Technology sector has undergone a **complete re-rating** over the past decade. The 2017 P/E peak of 132.5x has contracted to 60.9x in 2025, representing a **53% normalization**. The sector is now trading at valuations not seen since 2014-2015 era.

## Industry Deep Dive

### 1. Computer Software: Prepackaged Software

**The Most Dramatic Re-Rating**

| Metric | 2016 | 2017 (Peak) | 2019 | 2024 | 2025 | 2016→2025 Change |
|--------|------|-------------|------|------|------|------------------|
| **P/E** | 160.2x | **186.4x** | 77.7x | 42.7x | 56.0x | **-65.0%** |
| **P/S** | 21.1x | 18.2x | 13.8x | 8.3x | 6.8x | **-67.5%** |
| **P/B** | 19.4x | 17.2x | 14.6x | 7.2x | 9.0x | **-53.4%** |

**Analysis**: Software stocks experienced the most extreme valuation cycle:
- **2016-2017**: "Growth at any cost" era - P/E peaked at 186x
- **2018-2019**: Trade war concerns, early normalization to 78x
- **2020**: COVID accelerated digital transformation - held at 81x
- **2022**: Rate hike cycle - crashed to 62x
- **2024**: Growth stock trough - bottomed at 43x
- **2025**: **Recovery to 56x** - 31% rebound from 2024 lows

**2025 Signal**: The P/E rebound from 42.7x (2024) to 56.0x (2025) suggests:
1. Market has priced in a "soft landing" scenario
2. Quality software names are being rewarded for profitability
3. AI monetization is beginning to be reflected in multiples

**Opportunity**: Current P/E of 56x is still well below:
- 2017 peak: 186x (-70%)
- 2016 levels: 160x (-65%)
- Even 2019 pre-COVID: 78x (-28%)

This suggests software stocks are reasonably valued for long-term holders.

### 2. Semiconductors

**Cyclicality and AI Super- Cycle**

| Metric | 2016 | 2019 | 2020 | 2022 | 2024 | 2025 | 2016→2025 Change |
|--------|------|------|------|------|------|------|------------------|
| **P/E** | 98.7x | 127.7x (peak) | 103.3x | 63.4x (trough) | 87.7x | 78.1x | **-20.8%** |
| **P/S** | 16.2x | 18.1x (peak) | 17.8x | 11.1x (trough) | 12.0x | 11.4x | **-29.8%** |
| **P/B** | 14.7x | 9.9x | 10.7x | 9.2x | 8.2x | 8.2x | **-44.7%** |

**Analysis**: Semiconductors show classic cyclical patterns:
- **2019 Peak**: P/E 127.7x during pre-COVID chip shortage
- **2022 Trough**: P/E 63.4x during inventory correction, rate fears
- **2024-2025**: AI-driven recovery, P/E rebounded to 78-88x

**2025 Signal**: P/S at 11.4x is:
- Below 2016-2019 averages (13-18x)
- Above 2022 trough (11.1x)
- Suggests sustainable AI demand without extreme overvaluation

**Key Insight**: The P/B multiple has stabilized at 8.2x (2024-2025), indicating:
- Market expectations for normalized returns
- Unlike 2019 euphoria (P/B 9.9x), current levels reflect fundamentals
- Room for multiple expansion if AI growth accelerates

### 3. Retail: Computer Software & Peripheral Equipment

**The Value Play**

| Metric | 2016 | 2018 (trough) | 2020 | 2022 | 2024 | 2025 | 2016→2025 Change |
|--------|------|---------------|------|------|------|------|------------------|
| **P/E** | 33.2x | 25.7x | 26.0x | 31.0x | 34.2x | 23.0x | **-30.7%** |
| **P/S** | 5.3x | 4.7x (trough) | 7.6x | 8.5x | 8.2x | 8.4x | **+57.4%** |
| **P/B** | 7.2x | 5.4x (trough) | 4.3x (trough) | 10.2x | 8.7x | 6.5x | **-9.3%** |

**Analysis**: This is the **cheapest segment** by P/E throughout the entire period:
- **2016-2025**: P/E range of 23-37x vs. Software's 43-186x
- **2025**: P/E of 23.0x is the **lowest across all tech segments**

**Anomaly**: P/S surged from 4.3x (2020) to 8.5x (2022) but P/E remained low
- Indicates: Sales growth outpaced earnings
- 2024-2025: P/S stabilizing at 8.2-8.4x while P/E compressed to 23x
- Suggests: Margin pressure or reinvestment weighing on earnings

**2025 Signal**: Lowest P/E (23.0x) with decent P/S (8.4x) indicates:
- Either unloved value opportunity
- Or structural headwinds weighing on the sector
- Due diligence required on company-specific fundamentals

**Analysis**: Most consistent contraction without extreme volatility:
- **2016-2022**: Steady P/E decline from 89x to 30x
- **2022 Trough**: P/S 5.0x, P/E 29.6x - deeply oversold
- **2023-2025**: Gradual recovery to P/E 45x, P/S 4.5x

**2025 Signal**: P/S of 4.5x is the **lowest across all segments**, suggesting:
- Industrials are still undervalued relative to growth peers
- Manufacturing slowdown fears may be overdone
- Potential multiple expansion catalyst if macro improves

**Opportunity**: At 45x P/E and 4.5x P/S, this segment offers:
- Reasonable valuations for industrial tech exposure
- Potential leverage to economic recovery
- Lower downside than high-multiple segments

## Market Cycle Analysis

### Phase 1: Peak Exuberance (2016-2017)
- **Technology P/E**: 116x → 132x (+14%)
- **Software P/E**: 160x → 186x (+16%)
- **Drivers**: Low rates, quantitative easing, "growth at any cost"
- **Parallel**: Late-stage tech bull market similar to 1999-2000

### Phase 2: Early Normalization (2018-2019)
- **Technology P/E**: 108x → 95x (-12%)
- **Software P/E**: 151x → 78x (-48%)
- **Drivers**: Trade wars, Fed tightening, economic slowdown
- **Parallel**: Early 2000s post-bubble consolidation

### Phase 3: Pandemic Distortion (2020)
- **Technology P/E**: 95x → 74x (-22%)
- **Semiconductors P/E**: 128x → 103x (-20%)
- **Drivers**: COVID uncertainty, followed by digital acceleration
- **Note**: P/E compression due to earnings cliff, not multiple contraction

### Phase 4: Rate Shock Cycle (2022-2023)
- **Technology P/E**: 66x → 53x (-20%)
- **Software P/E**: 80x → 43x (-46%)
- **Semiconductors P/S**: 12.8x → 11.1x (-13%)
- **Drivers**: 525bps of Fed hikes, inflation fears, growth stock re-rating
- **Parallel**: 2018 "Powell pivot" and 2000-2002 bear market

### Phase 5: AI Re-Rating (2024-2025)
- **Software P/E**: 43x → 56x (+31%)
- **Semiconductors P/E**: 63x → 78x (+24%)
- **Technology P/E**: 53x → 61x (+14%)
- **Drivers**: AI monetization, soft-landing expectations, earnings growth
- **Key**: First meaningful multiple expansion since 2020

## Investment Implications

**Best Opportunities (Contracted Multiples):**

- **Semiconductors**: P/S 11.4x vs. 18.1x peak (2019) = 37% discount
- **Industrial Machinery**: P/E 45x vs. 89x (2016) = 50% discount

**Strategy**: Focus on quality companies with:
- Strong balance sheets
- Proven business models
- Real earnings (not just growth stories)

### 2. For Growth Investors

**Trend Reversal Signals (2024-2025):**
- **Software P/E**: +31% from 2024 trough (43x → 56x)
- **Semiconductors P/E**: +24% from 2023 trough (63x → 78x)
- **Technology P/E**: +14% from 2024 low (53x → 61x)

**Strategy**: The 2024-2025 re-rating suggests:
- Market has moved from "growth is dead" to "select growth works"
- AI leaders with real earnings are being rewarded
- Momentum may continue if earnings deliver

### 3. For Contrarian Investors

**Overlooked Value:**
- **Retail Software/Peripherals**: P/E 23x (lowest across all tech)
- **Industrial Machinery**: P/S 4.5x (cheapest price-to-sales)

**Strategy**: These segments may offer:
- Deep value opportunities
- Low expectations = high upside on positive surprises
- Defensive characteristics in uncertain markets

## Forward-Looking Indicators

### 1. Multiple Expansion Potential

From current 2025 levels:
- **Software** (56x): Room to 70-80x if AI monetization accelerates (+25-40%)
- **Semiconductors** (78x): Limited upside from here; fairly valued
- **Industrial Machinery** (45x): Most upside to 60-70x on macro recovery (+33-50%)

### 2. Multiple Contraction Risks

- **Software** at 56x: Still vulnerable to rate shocks, earnings disappointments
- **Semiconductors** at 78x: Downside to 60-65x if AI demand disappoints (-20%)
- **Technology** at 61x: Support at 50x if recession fears return (-18%)

### 3. Key Watching Points

**Bullish Signals:**
- Fed pivot / rate cuts → multiple expansion
- AI revenue acceleration → especially in Software
- Economic soft landing → Industrials re-rating

**Bearish Signals:**
- Fed holds higher for longer → pressure on high P/E
- AI overbuild / demand disappointment → Semis correction
- Recession → rotation to defensives, multiple compression

## Conclusion

The 10-year view (2016-2025) reveals a **complete market cycle** in Technology valuations:

1. **Peak Exuberance** (2016-2017): P/E 116-186x
2. **Normalization** (2018-2019): P/E 95-78x
3. **Pandemic** (2020): Earnings cliff, P/E distortion
4. **Rate Shock** (2022-2023): Trough at 53-63x
5. **AI Recovery** (2024-2025): Re-rating to 56-78x

**Current State (2025)**:
- Technology P/E: 61x (reasonable vs. history)
- Software P/E: 56x (70% below 2017 peak)
- Semiconductors P/E: 78x (fairly valued for AI growth)
- Industrials P/E: 45x (value opportunity)

**Investment Implication**:
- The 10-year contraction (-48% P/E) is largely complete
- 2024-2025 shows early signs of multiple expansion
- Current valuations support selective stock picking over passive exposure
- Quality companies in contracted-multiple segments offer best risk/reward
