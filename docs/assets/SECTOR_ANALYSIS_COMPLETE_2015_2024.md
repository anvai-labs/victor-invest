## Methodology Notes

**Data Sources:**
- SEC Company Facts API (filings, fundamentals)
- tickerdata (historical prices, split-adjusted)
- stock.symbol (sector/industry classification)

**Calculations:**
- P/E = Market Cap / Net Income (split-immune)
- P/S = Market Cap / Total Revenue (split-immune)
- P/B = Market Cap / Stockholders Equity (split-immune)

**Quality Controls:**
- Minimum 5 companies per sector median
- 5th-95th percentile outlier filtering
- Market cap consistency validation (excludes 20%+ mismatches)
- Split adjustment validation via `stock_splits` table

**Fiscal Year Notes:**
- Companies have different FY-ends (Dec 31, Jan 31, Mar 31, etc.)
- "FY 2024" = each company's own fiscal year (not calendar year)
- As of Feb 2025: FY 2024 is ~90% complete, FY 2025 is ~30% complete
