## Three Market Regimes

### Regime 1: Pre-COVID Expansion (2015-2019)
- **Characteristics:** Low volatility, steady multiples
- **P/E Median:** 28x
- **Top Performers:** Technology (+45%), Real Estate (+35%)
- **Bottom:** Energy (-25%)

### Regime 2: COVID Bubble (2020-2021)
- **Characteristics:** Extreme multiple expansion, then contraction
- **P/E Peak:** 74x (Technology, 2020)
- **Drivers:** Monetary stimulus, retail mania, growth stocks
- **Key Event:** March 2020 crash → rapid recovery

### Regime 3: Inflation & Rates (2022-2024)
- **Characteristics:** Multiple compression, value rotation
- **P/E Compression:** Tech -35%, Healthcare -26%
- **Winners:** Energy (+140% from 2022 lows), Financials
- **Losers:** Technology, Communication Services

## The Balloon Effect Framework

**Three ways multiples change:**

1. **Multiple Expansion** (Inflating)
   - Price ↑ faster than earnings
   - Usually bullish (driven by growth, rate cuts)

2. **Multiple Contraction** (Deflating)
   - Price ↑ slower than earnings (or ↓)
   - Usually bearish (driven by rates, recession fears)

3. **Stubborn** (Leaking)
   - Multiple stays flat despite news
   - Indicates market skepticism or structural headwinds

**Application:**
- NVDA 2023-24: Multiple 25x → 48x (Inflating)
- META 2021-23: Multiple 28x → 12x (Deflating)
- AAPL 2019-24: Multiple ~20x (Stubborn)

## Fiscal Year vs Calendar Year

**Important:** All data uses **Fiscal Year (FY)**, NOT calendar year

- **Company FY-ends:** Dec 31 (~65%), Jan 31 (retail), Mar 31, Jun 30, Sep 30
- **FY 2024:** Companies close books April 2024 → March 2025
- **Current status (Feb 2025):** FY 2024 is ~90% complete, FY 2025 is ~30% complete

**Implication:** When comparing "2024" across companies, you're comparing different 12-month periods based on each company's fiscal calendar.

## Data Quality Notes

- **Sample Size:** Minimum 5 companies per sector median
- **Outlier Exclusion:** 5th-95th percentile filtering applied
- **Split Adjustment:** P/E from aggregates (market_cap / net_income) - split-immune
- **Price Adjustment:** tickerdata prices are split-adjusted
- **Validation:** Market cap consistency check (excludes 20%+ mismatches)

## Methodology

**Sources:**
- SEC Company Facts API (filings, fundamentals)
- tickerdata (historical prices, split-adjusted)
- stock.symbol table (sector/industry classification)

**Calculations:**
- P/E = Market Cap / Net Income (split-immune)
- P/S = Market Cap / Total Revenue (split-immune)
- P/B = Market Cap / Stockholders Equity (split-immune)

**Validation:**
- Market cap ≈ Price × Shares (within 20% tolerance)
- Symbols failing validation excluded from medians
