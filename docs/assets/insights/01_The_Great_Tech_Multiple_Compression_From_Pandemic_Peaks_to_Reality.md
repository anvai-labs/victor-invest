# The Great Tech Multiple Compression: From Pandemic Peaks to Reality

**How Technology sector P/E multiples collapsed 73% from 2016 highs, yet found a new floor in 2024**


## The Story

The Technology sector witnessed one of the most dramatic valuation re-ratings in market history. From the dizzying heights of 2016-2020 (when investors paid 116x earnings for growth), the sector has undergone a massive compression.

### What Happened?

**2016-2020: The Growth-at-Any-Price Era**
- P/E multiples peaked at 116x in 2016
- P/S ratios reached 17.2x as investors flooded into growth stocks
- Money was free, and growth was the only metric that mattered

**2021-2022: The Bubble Bursts**
- P/E collapsed to 55.7x (-52% from peak)
- Rising interest rates exposed overvalued stocks
- The "growth at any cost" era ended abruptly

**2023-2024: Finding the New Normal**
- P/E stabilized around 31x in 2024
- Quality companies with real earnings survived
- The sector matured from speculation to fundamentals

### Key Takeaway

The Tech sector's P/E compression from 116x to 31x tells a story of market maturation. The sector didn't collapse—it grew into a more reasonable valuation. Companies with real earnings and cash flows (AAPL, MSFT) maintained or expanded their multiples, while speculative plays faced harsh reality.

### What's Next for 2025?

Early 2025 data shows a modest recovery to 32x P/E, suggesting the sector has found a bottom. The AI revolution (NVDA, AMD) is creating a new sub-cycle of valuation expansion, but this time it's backed by real revenue and earnings growth.
