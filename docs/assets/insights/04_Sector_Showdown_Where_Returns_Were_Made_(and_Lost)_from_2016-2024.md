## The Story

The market from 2016 to 2024 wasn't one story—it was a collection of vastly different narratives across sectors. Understanding these divergent paths is key to positioning for the next cycle.

### Three Clear Regimes

**Regime 1: The Value Re-rating (Energy)**
- Oil & gas companies went from "broken business model" to "cash cows"
- Commodity inflation + supply shocks = pricing power
- Dividends + buybacks = total return machine

**Regime 2: The Growth Correction (Tech)**
- 2016-2020: Investors paid anything for growth
- 2021-2024: Rising rates exposed overvaluation
- Outcome: 73% multiple compression, though many companies grew into it

**Regime 3: The Steady Compounders (Industrials)**
- Boring businesses with real earnings
- Benefited from infrastructure spending, reshoring
- Modest multiple expansion (+9%) + earnings growth = solid returns

### Key Takeaways

1. **Mean reversion works**: Energy at 7x was too cheap, Tech at 116x was too expensive
2. **Earnings matter**: Sectors with real earnings (Finance, Energy) held up better
3. **Rates matter**: Rising rates crushed duration assets (Tech, REITs, Telecom)
4. **Boring is beautiful**: Industrials quietly delivered solid returns

### What's Next for 2025?

Early data suggests the rotation continues:
- **Energy**: Still expanding (+26% YoY to 17.7x)
- **Technology**: Stabilized around 32x
- **Health Care**: Continuing compression (-6.5% YoY)

The market is always moving, but these long-term trends show the power of starting valuation. Buy cheap sectors, sell expensive ones. It's that simple—but not easy.
