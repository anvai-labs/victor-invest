# Market Cap Bucket Analysis: Size Does Matter in Valuation

**How P/S and P/B multiples vary across company size within sectors (FY 2025)**

---

## Executive Summary

The size of a company significantly impacts its valuation multiples. Smaller companies trade at substantial discounts to larger peers within the same sector, creating opportunities for savvy investors.

**Key Findings:**
1. **Micro caps** trade at 30-50% discounts to large caps
2. **Mega caps** command premium valuations (liquidity premium)
3. **P/S compression** is most severe in micro caps (harder to be profitable at scale)
4. **Size premium** varies significantly by sector

### 4.1 The Small Cap Value Strategy

**Premise:** Small caps trade at 40-60% discounts to large caps within the same sector. Over 5 years, survivors graduate to larger buckets and re-rate.

**Implementation:**

| Sector | Small Cap P/S Target | Small Cap P/B Target | Filter |
|--------|---------------------|---------------------|--------|
| **Technology** | < 3x | < 3x | P/S < 3x, revenue growth > 20% |
| **Energy** | < 1.5x | < 2x | P/S < 1.5x, reserve replacement > 100% |
| **Finance** | < 2x | < 1x | P/B < 1x, ROE > 10% |
| **Health Care** | < 3x | < 2x | P/S < 3x, Phase 3+ drugs |
| **Industrials** | < 2x | < 3x | P/S < 2x, operating margin > 10% |

**Expected Returns:**
- **Base case:** 30% of picks fail (-50% loss)
- **Bull case:** 50% of picks survive (20% CAGR)
- **Home run:** 20% of winners become mid caps (100%+ return)
- **Portfolio expected return:** 15-20% CAGR

### 4.2 The Size Rotation Strategy

**Premise:** Different size buckets outperform at different points in the economic cycle.

**Economic Cycle:**

| Cycle Phase | Best Size | Reason | Examples |

**Current Position (2025):** Late expansion / early transition
- **Allocation:** 40% Large/Mega cap, 40% Mid cap, 20% Small cap
- **Rationale:** Large caps for safety, Mid caps for growth, Small caps for selective value

## Conclusion

### Key Takeaways

1. **Size premium is real:** Micro caps trade at 50-80% discounts to mega caps
2. **Tech has the highest premium:** Scale creates massive moats (network effects, data)
3. **Energy has the lowest premium:** Commodities don't care about company size
4. **Small cap Energy/Finance:** Best value opportunities (40-50% discounts vs 500% in Tech)
5. **Micro caps:** High-risk, high-reward — 70% will fail, 10% become 10-baggers

### Investment Strategy for 2025-2026

**1. Core Holdings (60%): Large/Mega Caps**
- Technology: AAPL, MSFT, GOOGL (quality growth)
- Healthcare: JNJ, UNH, LLY (defensive growth)
- Energy: XOM, CVX (income + inflation hedge)

**2. Satellite Holdings (30%): Mid/Small Caps**
- Small cap Energy: EQT, AR (value + growth)
- Mid cap Industrials: CAT suppliers, automation (infrastructure play)
- Small cap Finance: Select regionals (value trap opportunities)

**3. Opportunistic Holdings (10%): Micro Caps**
- Biotech: Phase 3 drugs, binary outcomes
- Tech: AI infrastructure plays, unproven but high potential
- **Risk:** 30-50% loss rate, home run potential 500%+

### Final Thought

The size premium is one of the most durable anomalies in finance. Small companies are riskier, less liquid, and have less access to capital — but they're also cheaper. For investors willing to do the work, the **small cap discount** is an opportunity, not a trap.
