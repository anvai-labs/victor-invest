## Part 1: Technology - The Rise, Fall, and Stabilization

### Phase 1: Growth-at-Any-Cost Era (2016-2019)

**The Progression:**

| Year | P/S Multiple | YoY Change | Narrative | Driver |
|------|-------------|-----------|-----------|--------|
| **2016** | 17.2x | — | "Growth is scarce, pay up" | Fed 0.5%, QE |
| **2017** | ~18x | +5% | "Tax cuts coming, growth accelerates" | Trump tax cuts |
| **2018** | ~16x | -11% | **PIVOT:** Trade war fears emerge | Trade war begins |
| **2019** | ~15x | -6% | "Growth slowing but still premium" | Rate cuts begin |

**Key Insight:** This was the **revenue-only** era. Companies were valued on:
- **Revenue growth rate** (not profitability)
- **User growth** (DAU/MAU, not EPS)
- **Market opportunity** (TAM, not EBITDA)

**Representative Symbols (Revenue-Driven Valuations):**

| Symbol | 2016-2019 Story | Valuation Driver | Why P/S mattered |
|--------|----------------|-----------------|------------------|
| **SNAP** | IPO 2017 at $17 | User growth: 150M→210M DAU | Unprofitable, P/S only metric |
| **TWTR** | 2016: $16 → 2019: $32 | Revenue growth: +500% | Negative earnings, P/S 8x→12x |
| **SHOP** | 2016: $10 → 2019: $150 | GMV growth: +400% | Unprofitable, P/S 15x→35x |
| **ZM** | IPO 2019 at $80 | Revenue growth: +100% YoY | Unprofitable, P/S 40x |

**The Venture Capital Effect:**

In 2016-2019, public market valuations mirrored private VC metrics:
- **Burn rate** = Investment in growth
- **Negative margins** = "Growing into profitability"
- **P/S 20x+** = "Next Amazon/Google"

**Why This Era Ended:**
1. **2018 Trade War:** Showed growth isn't guaranteed
2. **2020 COVID:** Forced cash-flow realism
3. **2021-22 Rate Hikes:** Made "profitability later" expensive

### Phase 2: The COVID Peak (2020)

**The Pivot Moment:**

| Metric | Pre-COVID (2019) | COVID Low | COVID Peak (Dec 2020) |
|--------|------------------|-----------|----------------------|
| **Tech P/S** | ~15x | ~12x (Mar) | ~17x (Dec) |
| **Narrative** | "Growth premium" | "Sell everything" | "Digital transformation accelerated" |

**Why P/S Peaked in 2020:**

1. **Work-from-home accelerated digital adoption**
   - ZM, DOCU, Slack valuations went vertical
   - "Every company needs digital tools"

2. **Fed printed $4 trillion, rates at 0%**
   - Free money → growth stocks
   - Discount distant earnings less = higher multiples

3. **Retail investor boom (Robinhood)**
   - momentum > fundamentals
   - Growth stocks = lottery tickets

**Representative Symbols (2020 P/S Peak):**

| Symbol | 2019 P/S | 2020 Peak P/S | 2024 P/S | Story |
|--------|----------|---------------|----------|-------|
| **ZM** | 30x | 120x | 5x | COVID winner, then mean reverted |
| **DOCU** | 15x | 50x | 4x | E-signature boom, then normalized |
| **SHOP** | 35x | 60x | 12x | E-commerce surge, then competition |
| **CRWD** | 20x | 40x | 15x | Cybersecurity demand, then saturated |

**Key Insight:** **2020 was the P/S peak** because revenue growth looked infinite, but earnings didn't exist. When rates rose in 2022, the music stopped.

### Phase 3: The Great Compression (2021-2024)

**The Progressive Collapse:**

| Year | P/S Multiple | YoY Change | Fed Funds Rate | Pivot Event |
|------|-------------|-----------|-----------------|-------------|
| **2021** | ~12x | -29% | 0-0.25% | Inflation fears begin |
| **2022** | ~8x | -33% | 0.25% → 4.5% | **MAJOR PIVOT:** Rate hikes begin |
| **2023** | ~5x | -38% | 4.5% → 5.5% | "Higher for longer" settles in |
| **2024** | ~3x | -40% | 5.25-5.5% | Market adjusts to new normal |

**Why P/S Collapsed 83% (17x → 3x):**

1. **Rates rose from 0% to 5.5%**
   - Discount rate ↑ → Present value ↓
   - "Profitability later" became "Profitability now"

2. **Revenue growth slowed**
   - 2016-2020: Tech revenue grew 30-50% YoY
   - 2021-2024: Tech revenue grew 10-20% YoY
   - Lower growth = lower P/S multiple

3. **Earnings emerged**
   - Companies transitioned from "grow at all costs" to "profitable growth"
   - P/S became less relevant, P/E became more relevant

**Representative Symbols (P/S Compression Victims):**

| Symbol | 2020 Peak P/S | 2024 P/S | Compression | Earnings Story |
|--------|---------------|----------|-------------|----------------|
| **SNAP** | 25x | 3x | -88% | Still unprofitable, P/S only metric |
| **PIN** | 30x | 4x | -87% | Pinterest growth slowed |
| **ROKU** | 10x | 2x | -80% | Streaming competition |
| **TWLO** | 20x | 3x | -85% | API platform saturated |

**The Winners (Quality at Reasonable Price):**

| Symbol | 2020 P/S | 2024 P/S | Earnings Margin | Why It Survived |
|--------|----------|----------|-----------------|-----------------|
| **AAPL** | 7x | 10x | 25% net margin | Real earnings, P/E 30x justified |
| **MSFT** | 10x | 12x | 35% net margin | Cloud profits, not just revenue |
| **GOOGL** | 6x | 8x | 25% net margin | Search monopoly = cash machine |
| **META** | 8x | 8x | 30% net margin | "Year of Efficiency" = margins ↑ |

**Key Insight:** The P/S compression revealed the **quality divide**:
- **Revenue-only companies:** P/S 20x → 3x (crashed)
- **Profitable companies:** P/S 10x → 10x (stable or expanded)

### Phase 4: The Stabilization (2024-2025)

**The New Normal:**

| Metric | 2024 | 2025 | Change | Trend |
|--------|------|------|--------|-------|
| **Tech P/S** | 3.0x | 3.9x | +30% | **PIVOT:** Stabilizing |
| **Tech P/E** | 31.3x | 32.4x | +4% | Found floor |
| **Fed Funds** | 5.25-5.5% | 4.75-5.0% | -0.5% | Rate cuts begin? |

**Why Tech Stabilized:**

1. **Earnings caught up**
   - 2016: Tech earnings (aggregate) = $100B
   - 2024: Tech earnings (aggregate) = $500B
   - Market can now justify 32x P/E on real earnings

2. **AI boom created new growth narrative**
   - NVDA: 2023 $27B revenue → 2024 $61B revenue (+125%)
   - AMD, AVGO, MRVL: AI infrastructure demand
   - This is **revenue-backed growth**, not hype

3. **Quality over growth**
   - Market now rewards: Profitability + Cash Flow + Buybacks
   - Not just: Revenue growth + User growth + "We'll monetize later"

**Representative Symbols (2024-2025 Stabilization):**

| Symbol | 2024 P/S | 2025 P/S | Earnings Growth | Story |
|--------|----------|----------|------------------|-------|
| **AAPL** | 10x | 10x | +10% | Cash machine, P/S stable |
| **MSFT** | 12x | 12x | +15% | Cloud + AI = growth re-rate |
| **GOOGL** | 8x | 8x | +12% | Cheap vs history, steady compounder |
| **NVDA** | 25x | 25x | +200% | AI bubble within Tech (100x P/E) |

**Key Insight:** Tech stabilized at **P/S ~4x, P/E ~32x**. This is the new normal:
- **Not 2016:** P/S 17x (bubble)
- **Not 2020:** P/S 12x (COVID peak)
- **2024-2025:** P/S 4x, P/E 32x (quality at reasonable price)

**The Low Point:**

| Metric | 2019 | 2020 Low | 2020 High | Change |
|--------|------|----------|-----------|--------|

*Many Energy companies had negative earnings in 2020

**Why Energy Collapsed:**

1. **COVID demand shock**
   - Global lockdowns → oil demand -30%
   - Futures went negative (storage full)

2. **ESG movement peak**
   - "Oil is dead", "Peak oil demand"
   - Investors divested from fossil fuels

3. **Oversupply from 2014-2019**
   - US shale boom → oversupply
   - OPEC price war with Russia (March 2020)

**Key Insight:** 2020 was the **capitulation point**. Energy P/S 0.8x, dividend cuts, ESG headlines = nobody wanted to own oil.

### Phase 2: The Re-Rating Begins (2021-2022)

**The Progressive Recovery:**

| Year | P/S Multiple | YoY Change | Oil Price | Narrative Pivot |
|------|-------------|-----------|-----------|-----------------|
| **2021** | 1.1x | +38% | $70/bbl | "Demand recovering" |
| **2022** | 1.3x | +18% | $100/bbl | **PIVOT:** Russia-Ukraine war = supply shock |
| **2023** | 1.4x | +8% | $80/bbl | "Structural underinvestment" |
| **2024** | 1.4x | 0% | $75/bbl | "New normal, higher floor" |
| **2025** | 1.4x | 0% | $80/bbl | "Cash cow, not growth" |

**Why Energy Re-Rated (+75% from 0.8x to 1.4x):**

1. **Supply shock from Russia-Ukraine war**
   - Russian oil sanctioned → supply -2M bbl/day
   - Oil spiked to $120/bbl (2022)

2. **Underinvestment = supply constraint**
   - 2015-2020: ESG + low prices → capex cuts
   - 2021-2025: Demand recovered, supply didn't
   - Result: Pricing power for producers

3. **Capital allocation revolution**
   - 2020: "Drill baby drill" → value destruction
   - 2025: "Dividends + buybacks" → shareholder-friendly
   - XOM 2025: $15B buyback + 3.5% dividend

**Representative Symbols (Re-Rating Winners):**

| Symbol | 2020 P/S | 2024 P/S | Expansion | 2020-2024 Return |
|--------|----------|----------|-----------|------------------|

| **CVX** | 0.9x | 1.7x | +89% | +220% |
| **COP** | 0.6x | 2.7x | +350% | +350% |
| **SLB** | 1.0x | 2.0x | +100% | +400% |
| **EOG** | 1.5x | 2.9x | +93% | +86% |

**Key Insight:** Energy re-rated from **"broken business model"** to **"cash cow"**. P/S went from 0.8x (too cheap) to 1.4x (fair value for commodity producer).

## Part 3: Finance - The Value Trap (2016-2025)

### The Stagnation Story

**The Flat Line:**

| Year | P/E Multiple | YoY Change | Fed Funds Rate | Narrative |
|------|-------------|-----------|-----------------|-----------|
| **2016** | ~45x | — | 0.5% | Post-GFC overvaluation |
| **2018** | ~15x | -67% | 2.5% | **PIVOT:** Normalize to reality |
| **2020** | 15.2x | +1% | 0-0.25% | COVID panic, too cheap |
| **2022** | 15.2x | 0% | 4.5% | "Rates rising = banks profit" (didn't happen) |
| **2024** | 14.5x | -5% | 5.25-5.5% | Yield curve inverted = pressure |
| **2025** | 13.5x | -7% | 4.75-5.0% | Getting CHEAPER |

**Why Finance Didn't Re-Rate (10 Years at ~15x):**

1. **Post-GFC trauma (2008-2016)**
   - Investors remember: Banks can go to zero
   - Regulatory overhang (Dodd-Frank, stress tests)

2. **Yield curve flattening/inversion**
   - Banks borrow short, lend long
   - Inverted yield curve = margin compression
   - 2022-2024: 10Y-2Y = -100bps (bad for banks)

3. **Tech competition**
   - Fintech (Stripe, Plaid) eating payment margins
   - Neobanks (Chime, Revolut) eating deposit growth

**Representative Symbols (The Quality Divide):**

| Symbol | 2016 P/E | 2025 P/E | 2016-2025 Return | Why It Worked (or didn't) |
|--------|----------|----------|-------------------|---------------------------|
| **JPM** | 11x | 12x | +200% | Quality compounder, market share gains |
| **BAC** | 12x | 11x | +150% | Turnaround story, core deposits |
| **WFC** | 13x | 10x | +50% | **Value trap:** Scandal overhang |
| **GS** | 10x | 10x | +100% | Trading volatility, episodic |
| **SCHW** | 18x | 15x | +120% | Discount broker, rate sensitivity |

**Key Insight:** Finance at 15x P/E for 10 years = **structural discount**. Either:
- **Value trap:** Market will NEVER pay more than 15x for banks
- **Opportunity:** Mean reversion eventually happens (rates cut, yield curve steepens)

## Part 6: Lessons for Investors

### Lesson 1: Revenue-Driven Sectors Crash Harder When Rates Rise

**2016-2020 (Free Money Era):**
- Tech P/S: 17x (pay anything for growth)
- Narrative: "Profitability is obsolete"

**2021-2024 (Expensive Money Era):**
- Tech P/S: 3x (-83% compression)
- Narrative: "Profitability matters"

**The Takeaway:** When you buy revenue-driven sectors (Tech, Biotech, SaaS):
- **In low-rate environments:** P/S 20x might be justified
- **In high-rate environments:** P/S 5x is expensive
- **Rule of thumb:** P/S should be ≤ (revenue growth rate) / (interest rate)

### Lesson 2: Earnings-Driven Sectors Are More Resilient

**Finance (Earnings-Driven, P/E Stable):**
- 2016-2025: P/E stayed at ~15x
- Why: Banks have real earnings, dividends, book value
- Multiple doesn't expand much, but doesn't crash either

**Energy (Commodity-Driven, P/S +75% Re-Rating):**
- 2020: P/S 0.8x (too cheap)
- 2024: P/S 1.4x (fair value)
- Why: Real earnings, cash flows, dividends

**The Takeaway:** Sectors with real earnings (Finance, Energy, Industrials) have:
- Lower volatility (multiples don't swing 80%)
- Lower upside (don't re-rate +300%)
- Lower downside (don't crash -80%)
- **Better for conservative investors**

### Lesson 3: Pivot Points Create Opportunities

**Buy at Pivot Points:**

| Pivot Point | When | What to Buy | Why |
|-------------|------|-------------|-----|
| **Trade War (2018)** | Tech sells off -20% | Quality Tech (AAPL, MSFT) | Growth panic = buying opportunity |
| **COVID Crash (2020)** | Everything sells off | Quality (JPM), Value (XOM) | Panic creates bargains |
| **Rate Hikes Begin (2022)** | Growth stocks crash | Value sectors (Energy, Finance) | Rotation from growth to value |
| **Fed Pivot (2024-25?)** | Rates cut | Rate-sensitive (Tech, Finance) | Growth stocks rally |

**Sell at Pivot Points:**

| Pivot Point | When | What to Sell | Why |
|-------------|------|--------------|-----|
| **COVID Peak (Dec 2020)** | Tech P/S at 20x | Unprofitable growth (ZM, DOCU) | Bubble valuations |
| **Rate Hikes (2022)** | Free money ends | Revenue-only tech (SNAP, PIN) | P/S compression inevitable |
| **AI Hype (2024-25?)** | NVDA at 100x P/E | AI bubble stocks | Earnings can't catch up |

### Lesson 4: Know Which Metric Matters for Your Sector

**P/E Sectors (Earnings-Driven):**
- Finance, Energy, Industrials, Consumer Staples
- Focus: EPS growth, margins, dividends
- Multiple range: 10-30x (stable)

**P/S Sectors (Revenue-Driven):**
- Technology (unprofitable), Biotech, E-commerce, SaaS
- Focus: Revenue growth, user growth, market share
- Multiple range: 2-20x (highly volatile)

**P/B Sectors (Asset-Driven):**
- Finance (banks), Real Estate (REITs)
- Focus: Book value, ROE, dividend yield
- Multiple range: 0.8-2.5x (stable)

1. **2016-2018:** Growth era peak → trade war fears
2. **2020:** COVID crash → free money peak
3. **2022:** Rate hikes begin → free money ends
4. **2023:** "Higher for longer" settles in

- **Energy:** Earnings-driven, P/S re-rated +75% (0.8x → 1.4x) from too cheap
- **Finance:** Earnings-driven, stuck at 15x P/E for 10 years (value trap?)
