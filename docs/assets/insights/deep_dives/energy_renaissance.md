# The Energy Renaissance: From Pandemic Collapse to Record Profits

**How Oil & Gas companies saw P/E multiples triple while the rest of the market contracted**


## The Story

While Technology was contracting, the Energy sector was staging one of the most remarkable comebacks in market history. From a P/E of 7.3x in 2022 (during the pandemic demand collapse), the sector tripled to 14.1x by 2024.

### What Happened?

**2020-2021: The Pandemic Hit**
- Oil demand collapsed as lockdowns paralyzed the global economy
- P/E multiples hit rock bottom at 7.3x
- Many companies cut dividends and struggled to survive

**2022-2023: The Supercycle Begins**
- Russia-Ukraine war disrupted global supply
- Inflation drove commodity prices to record highs
- Energy companies printed money

**2024-2025: The New Normal**
- P/E stabilized at 14-18x range
- Companies returned cash to shareholders via buybacks and dividends
- ESG investing rotated back to energy security

**Key Insight:** Energy's "renaissance" was driven by:
1. **2021-2022:** Oil price spike from COVID recovery + Russia-Ukraine war → earnings boom
2. **2023-2025:** Normalization as supply adjusted and costs increased → EPS declined
3. **P/E re-rating:** From depressed 7-9x (2022) to 17.7x (2025) as market accepted Energy as cash-generative sector

The "multiple expansion" from 7.3x to 17.7x (+143%) is largely driven by earnings normalization (from negative/low to positive) rather than pure valuation expansion.

### Key Takeaway

The Energy sector's +93% P/E expansion from 2022 to 2024 wasn't about growth—it was about re-rating. In 2022, investors priced these companies like they were going out of business. By 2024, they realized Energy was essential and printing cash.

### What's Different This Time?

Unlike previous oil booms, today's Energy companies are:
1. **Disciplined**: Not drilling uneconomic wells
2. **Capital Return**: 70%+ of cash flow returned to shareholders
3. **Low Valuation**: Still 50% discount to market multiple
4. **Energy Security**: Geopolitics made domestic production strategic

### What's Next for 2025?

Early 2025 data shows P/E expanding further to 17.7x (+25% YoY) as investors rotate into value. The sector trades at a significant discount to its historical average despite record profits.
