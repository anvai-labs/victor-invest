# Healthcare: The Defensive Sector That Became a Value Play

**From pandemic darling to value trap, Healthcare offers quality at depressed prices**


## The Story

Healthcare used to be the ultimate defensive sector—steady earnings regardless of economic conditions. But from 2016 to 2024, the sector has quietly become a value play, with P/E compressing from ~60x to 27.6x (-54%).

### What Happened?

**2016-2019: The Biotech Boom**
- P/E averaged 60x+ as investors chased biotech dreams
- Gene therapy, CAR-T, immuno-oncology drove massive valuations
- Many companies had no revenue, let alone profits

**2020-2021: The COVID Dividend**
- Vaccine makers became household names
- PFE, MRNA, JNJ delivered in a global crisis
- Investors rewarded the sector with premium multiples

**2022-2024: The Hangover**
- Interest rates crushed unprofitable biotech
- P/E compression from 31x to 27.6x (-12% YoY)
- Quality shifted to profitable, cash-generative businesses

### Key Takeaway

Healthcare's -54% P/E compression from 2016 hides a more nuanced story. The sector split in two:
- **Winners**: Diabetes/obesity drugs (LLY, NVS) trade at 40x+
- **Losers**: Traditional biotech trades at 10x or less

The sector median of 27.6x masks this incredible dispersion.

### Why Healthcare is Interesting Now

1. **Demographics**: Aging population drives demand
2. **Innovation**: GLP-1 drugs, gene therapy, CAR-T
3. **Valuation**: Trades at discount to historical average
4. **Defensive**: Earns regardless of economic conditions

### What's Next for 2025?

Early 2025 data shows further compression to 25.8x P/E (-6.5% YoY). The sector is getting cheaper even as fundamentals improve. This could be the setup for a mean reversion trade.
