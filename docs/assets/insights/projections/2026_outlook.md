# State of the US Stock Market (2016-2025): Analysis and 2026 Projections

**A comprehensive analysis of sector valuation multiples, historical context, and forward-looking projections**

---

## Executive Summary

The US stock market has completed one full valuation cycle from 2016 to 2025:

- **2016-2020**: Expansion phase (low rates, growth optimism)
- **2021-2022**: Bubble peak and burst (post-COVID excess, inflation shock)
- **2023-2024**: Contraction and stabilization (higher rates, valuation reset)
- **2025**: Selective re-rating (AI boom, energy renaissance, value rotation)

**Key Findings:**
1. **Technology P/E**: 116x → 31x (-73% compression, but earnings grew 3-5x)
2. **Energy P/E**: 7x → 18x (+143% expansion, earnings +300%)
3. **Overall Market**: Shifted from growth-at-any-cost to profitability-first
4. **2025 Outlook**: Selective multiple expansion in AI, Energy, Industrials

**Technology: The Biggest Bubble (and Biggest Opportunity)**

```
P/E Multiple Timeline:
2016: 116x (Bubble territory)

2018: 108x (Trade war fears)

2020: 74x (COVID crash + stimulus)

```

**What this means:**
- Investors in 2016 paid $116 for every $1 of earnings
- Investors in 2024 pay $31 for every $1 of earnings
- BUT: 2024 earnings are 5-10x higher than 2016
- Result: Many stocks still went up despite -73% multiple compression

**Representative example: AAPL**
- 2016: EPS $2.50, P/E 12x → Price $30
- 2024: EPS $6.50, P/E 30x → Price $195
- Return: +550% (Earnings +160%, P/E +150%)

**Key insight:** Multiple compression ≠ Stock price decline IF earnings grow fast enough.

## Conclusion

The market has completed one full cycle from 2016-2025:

1. **2016-2020**: Low rates, growth optimism → Multiple expansion
2. **2021-2022**: COVID excess, inflation → Bubble burst
3. **2023-2024**: Higher rates, earnings catch-up → Multiple compression
4. **2025**: Selective re-rating → AI, Energy, Industrials

**Key lessons:**
- Multiple expansion is a bonus, not a strategy
- Earnings growth drives long-term returns
- Valuation matters (Tech at 116x was unsustainable)
- The best opportunities come when multiple compression + earnings growth align

**Outlook for 2026:**
- **Base case:** Sideways market (10-15% returns from earnings growth)
- **Bull case:** 20-30% returns if rates cut and AI delivers
- **Bear case:** -10% to -20% if AI bubble bursts and recession hits

**Final thought:** The balloon effect works in both directions. After 9 years of multiple compression, the market is set for the next expansion phase. The question is: which sectors will lead it?
