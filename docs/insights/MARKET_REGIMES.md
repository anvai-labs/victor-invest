# Market Regimes (2015-2024)

**Data Source:** SEC company facts + market data
**Analysis Date:** February 2025

---

## Three Market Regimes

### Regime 1: Pre-COVID Expansion (2015-2019)

**Characteristics:** Low volatility, steady growth

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Duration | 5 years |
| Fed Funds | 0.25% → 2.50% |
| P/E Median | 28x |
| Best Performer | Technology (+45%) |
| Worst Performer | Energy (-25%) |

**Key Events:**
- Trade tensions (China-US)
- Gradual rate hikes (2017-2018)
- Tax cuts (2017)
- No significant bubbles

**Market Behavior:**
- Classic economic cycle
- Growth stocks outperformed
- Energy sector declined (oil oversupply)

---

### Regime 2: COVID Bubble (2020-2021)

**Characteristics:** Extreme volatility, policy-driven

| Year | Fed Funds | P/E Peak | Key Event |
|------|-----------|----------|-----------|
| 2020 | 0% → 0.25% | 74x (Tech) | COVID crash, emergency cuts |
| 2021 | 0.25% | 57x (Tech) | Stimulus, vaccine, inflation begins |

**Key Events:**
- Feb 2020: COVID crash, Fed cuts to ZERO
- Mar 2020: $5T+ stimulus announced
- Nov 2020: Vaccine news → rotation to cyclicals
- Nov 2021: Fed taper announcement → inflation concerns

**Winners:** Technology, Communication Services, Consumer Discretionary
**Losers:** Energy (initially), Financials (bank stress)

---

### Regime 3: Inflation & Rates (2022-2024)

**Characteristics:** Multiple compression, value rotation

| Year | Fed Funds | Key Event |
|------|-----------|-----------|
| 2022 | 0% → 4.5% | Fastest hiking cycle ever |
| 2023 | 4.5% → 5.25% | Banking crisis (SVB) |
| 2024 | 5.25% → ~4.5% | AI boom, soft landing hopes |

**Sector Performance:**
- Technology: 73x → 48x (-27%)
- Healthcare: 52x → 33x (-36%)
- Energy: 7x → 14x (+100% from bottom)
- Financials: 11x → 19x (+73%)
- Real Estate: 30x → 43x (+43%)

**Key Pivot Points:**
- Feb 2022: Ukraine invasion → energy spike
- Mar 2023: SVB failure → financial stress
- Mar 2024: AI enthusiasm → tech multiple expansion
- Late 2024: Rate cut hopes → relief rally

## The Balloon Effect

### Inflating Multiple (NVDA 2023-24)
```
Earnings +100% → Price +50% → Multiple EXPANDS → Price +200%
```
Market rewards growth with multiple expansion.

### Deflating Multiple (META 2021-23)
```
Earnings +50% → Price -20% → Multiple COMPRESSES → Price -40%
```
Market loses confidence despite earnings growth.

### Stubborn Multiple (AAPL 2019-24)
```
Earnings +150% → Price +150% → Multiple STAYS FLAT
```
Market prices efficiency—multiple unchanged.

## Investment Implications by Regime

### Post-Regime 2 (COVID Bubble)
- Growth stocks overvalued, vulnerable to rate hikes
- Value stocks overlooked, set for rotation
- Inflation emerging as real threat

### Post-Regime 3 (Rate Hikes)
- Growth multiples compressed, finding support
- Value stocks outperformed (Energy +140%)
- Financials recovering from 2023 lows
- Tech finding new growth (AI)

### Looking Forward (2025+)
- Rate cuts expected → growth multiple relief
- Quality mega-caps maintain premium
- Small caps poised for rebound if rates decline
- AI beneficiaries vs. lags within sectors

## Regime Indicators to Watch

**Signaling Regime Change:**
- Fed policy pivot (tightening → easing or vice versa)
- Inflation breaking (sustained < 2% or > 5%)
- Yield curve inversion (2s-10s)
- Market leadership rotation (growth ↔ value)

**Current Status (2025):**
- Fed in pause/hold mode
- Inflation moderating but sticky
- Yield curve normalizing
- Tech leadership (AI-driven)
