# Sector Multiples Timeline Analysis (2020-2024)

## Executive Summary

This analysis tracks valuation multiples (P/E, P/S, P/B) across Technology sector and related industries from fiscal year 2020 to 2024, identifying multiple expansion (swelling) and contraction (shrinking) trends.

## Key Findings

### Technology Sector Overview

| Metric | 2020 | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 | 2020→2024 Change | Trend |
|--------|------|------|------|------|------------------|-------|
| **P/E** | 73.5x | 55.7x | 66.0x | 53.4x | **-27.3%** | SHRINKING |
| **P/S** | 11.3x | 9.8x | 9.8x | 8.7x | **-23.0%** | SHRINKING |
| **P/B** | 11.2x | 10.2x | 9.8x | 9.2x | **-18.0%** | SHRINKING |

**Interpretation**: Technology sector has experienced significant multiple contraction since 2020, with all three metrics showing downward trends. This suggests the sector is becoming more attractive from a valuation perspective compared to pandemic-era peaks.

## Industry Breakdown

### 1. Semiconductors

| Metric | 2020 | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 | Change | Trend |
|--------|------|------|------|------|--------|-------|
| **P/E** | 103.3x | 63.4x | 63.2x | 87.7x | **-15.2%** | Shrinking |
| **P/S** | 17.8x | 11.1x | 12.8x | 12.0x | **-32.6%** | Shrinking |
| **P/B** | 10.7x | 9.2x | 7.4x | 8.2x | **-23.3%** | Shrinking |

**Analysis**: Semiconductors show significant P/S compression (-32.6%) despite recent AI boom. The P/E rebound in 2024 (87.7x from 63.2x) suggests earnings are catching up with valuations. The sector remains expensive on absolute terms but is improving relative to 2020 peaks.

**Notable**: 2023 trough in P/B (7.4x) followed by 2024 recovery (8.2x) indicates cyclical bottom may have passed.

| **P/S** | 10.8x | 10.5x | 9.5x | 8.3x | **-23.0%** | Shrinking |
| **P/B** | 11.1x | 8.4x | 9.2x | 7.2x | **-35.3%** | Shrinking |

**Analysis**: Software has experienced the most dramatic P/E compression (-47.2%), indicating a major repricing of growth stocks. The 2023 P/E spike (80.5x) followed by 2024 collapse (42.7x) suggests market sentiment shifted from growth-at-all-costs to profitability focus.

**Opportunity**: Current P/E of 42.7x is near 5-year lows, potentially attractive for high-quality names with strong fundamentals.

| **P/E** | 70.0x | 43.6x | 31.1x | 89.8x | **+28.3%** | SWELLING |
| **P/S** | 6.6x | 5.4x | 7.8x | 7.4x | **+11.7%** | Swelling |
| **P/B** | 5.4x | 12.0x | 9.6x | 11.3x | **+111.4%** | SWELLING |

**Analysis**: Electronic Components is the **only group showing significant multiple expansion**. The dramatic P/B surge (+111.4%) and 2024 P/E spike (89.8x) suggest strong investor interest, likely driven by electrification trends and supply chain restructuring.

**Caution**: Swelling multiples indicate the group may be overheating. Consider waiting for normalization before new entries.

## Investment Implications

### Multiple Contraction (Shrinking) - Potential Opportunities

Groups showing significant multiple contraction may present value opportunities if fundamentals remain intact:

1. **Computer Software** (P/E -47.2%)
   - Growth stocks repriced to more reasonable levels
   - Focus on companies with strong profitability and cash flow

2. **Semiconductors** (P/S -32.6%)
   - Cyclical industry showing signs of recovery
   - AI tailwind supports long-term demand

3. **Technology Sector** (P/E -27.3%, P/S -23.0%)
   - Broad sector becoming more attractive
   - Quality large-cap names at reasonable valuations

### Multiple Expansion (Swelling) - Exercise Caution

Groups showing significant multiple expansion may be overheated:

1. **Electronic Components** (P/B +111.4%, P/E +28.3%)
   - Strong momentum but elevated valuations
   - Consider waiting for pullback

2. **Retail: Computer Software** (P/B +100.9%)
   - Recovering from depressed levels
   - May have further room to run but monitor closely

### Stable Multiples - Defensive Plays

1. **Industrial Machinery/Components**
   - Minimal multiple change indicates resilience
   - Suitable for conservative portfolios

2. **EDP Services** (P/E +3.5%)
   - High volatility but roughly stable long-term
   - Requires careful timing due to earnings sensitivity

## Recommendations

### For Growth Investors

- **Focus**: Computer Software (post-contraction), Semiconductors (cyclically undervalued)
- **Avoid**: Electronic Components (overheated)
- **Strategy**: Look for quality companies in contracting multiple groups

### For Value Investors

- **Focus**: Retail Computer Software (lowest P/E with moderate expansion)
- **Avoid**: EDP Services (volatile, unpredictable)
- **Strategy**: Patient accumulation during market weakness

### For Defensive Investors

- **Focus**: Industrial Machinery (stable multiples)
- **Avoid**: High-volatility groups (EDP Services, Semiconductors)
- **Strategy**: Dividend-paying companies with steady earnings

---

## Conclusion

The Technology sector and related industries have undergone significant multiple re-pricing since 2020. The broad trend shows **multiple contraction** making stocks more attractive from a valuation perspective. However, certain sub-sectors (Electronic Components, Retail Software) show **multiple expansion** indicating investor rotation.

**Key Takeaway**: The current environment favors **selective stock picking** over broad sector exposure. Focus on quality companies in groups with contracted multiples rather than chasing momentum in overheated segments.
